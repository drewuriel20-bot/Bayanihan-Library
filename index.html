Our mission

A digital bridge for Kaban ng Hiyas Library

Libralend was developed to modernize how residents interact with the beloved Kaban ng Hiyas Library. We believe that access to books and educational resources should be seamless and equitable.

Today, our catalog houses over 2,400 titles across fiction, non-fiction, Filipino literature, and academic references — all easily searchable and free to borrow for every registered community member.

Terms of Service

1. System Usage & Eligibility

Libralend is designed exclusively for the residents of Mandaluyong City and registered members of the Kaban ng Hiyas Library. Every member is strictly responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their account credentials and for all activities that occur under their account.

2. Borrowing Policy & Limitations

A registered user may borrow a maximum of five (5) books at any given time. The standard loan period is fourteen (14) days. Users are permitted to renew a borrowed book once through the Libralend online portal, provided there are no pending hold requests from other members for that specific title.

3. Lost, Damaged, and Overdue Materials

Any physical library material that is damaged or lost while checked out must be reported immediately to the Kaban ng Hiyas library administration. Failure to return borrowed books on or before the due date will result in an automatic, temporary suspension of the user's borrowing privileges until the overdue items are returned.

Privacy Policy

In compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173)

Information We Collect

To provide a seamless library lending experience, Libralend collects necessary personal information including your full name, email address, residential address, and system-generated reading history.

How We Use Your Data

The collected data is utilized strictly for account management, tracking borrowed materials, managing hold queues, and generating anonymous data analytics for our academic research (Thesis). Libralend will never sell, rent, or distribute your personal information to third-party entities.

Data Security

We implement standard technical and organizational security measures to protect your account and personal details against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.
