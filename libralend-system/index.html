Our story

A library built by the barangay, for the barangay

Bayanihan Community Library was founded in 2008 by a group of local teachers, parents, and retirees who believed that access to books should never depend on income or age.

Today, we house over 2,400 titles across fiction, non-fiction, Filipino literature, children's books, and academic references — all free to borrow for every registered community member.

We survive on donations, volunteer hours, and the love of our community.
