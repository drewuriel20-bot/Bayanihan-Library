Our mission

A digital bridge for Kaban ng Hiyas Library

Libralend was developed to modernize how residents interact with the beloved Kaban ng Hiyas Library. We believe that access to books and educational resources should be seamless and equitable.

Today, our catalog houses over 2,400 titles across fiction, non-fiction, Filipino literature, and academic references — all easily searchable and free to borrow for every registered community member.
